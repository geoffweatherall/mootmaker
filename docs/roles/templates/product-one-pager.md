# <Thing being proposed>

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Rejected — <date>

## The problem

What is wrong or missing today, from a user's point of view. Not the solution.

## Who has this problem

Which of mootmaker's audiences — someone trying the demo, or someone evaluating the developer.
If the honest answer is "neither, it just sounds interesting", say so; that is still a valid
reason here, but it should be named rather than dressed up.

## What we would do

Two or three sentences. Enough to picture it, not enough to design it.

## Why now, or why not

What makes this worth doing ahead of everything else on the board. If nothing does, this is a
backlog item rather than a proposal.

## What it costs

Running cost against scale-to-zero, and rough build effort. A recurring cost needs a
better reason than a one-off.

## What we are not doing

The adjacent things this deliberately excludes. Usually the most useful section.

## Decision

Accepted or rejected, by whom, on what date, and why. A rejected one-pager is worth keeping —
it stops the same idea being re-argued from scratch in six months.
