# Document templates

Shapes for documents some hats produce repeatedly. Copy one, fill it in, delete what does not
apply — they are starting points, not forms to complete.

| Template | Hat | For |
|---|---|---|
| [product-one-pager.md](product-one-pager.md) | [Product owner](../product-owner.md) | Arguing for or against building something, before it becomes a design |
| [marketing-brief.md](marketing-brief.md) | [Marketer](../marketer.md) | Positioning a feature or the product for a specific audience |
| [release-note.md](release-note.md) | [Product owner](../product-owner.md) | Describing what changed, for a non-technical reader |
| [incident-note.md](incident-note.md) | [Operator](../operator.md) | Recording something that broke, leaked, or cost money unexpectedly |

A design document has its own template, in [`../../../designs/README.md`](../../../designs/README.md).
