# <Feature or campaign>

## Audience

Which audience, specifically. "Everyone" means the copy will land with nobody.

## The one sentence

If a reader takes away exactly one thing, this is it. Write it before anything else; if it is
hard, the positioning is not clear yet.

## What it actually does

Traceable to `../../reference/business-functionality.md`. Every claim here must be true of the
deployed system today.

## Why it matters to them

The benefit, not the mechanism. "Never double-book a room" rather than "conflict detection".

## Proof

What a sceptical reader can check — the live demo, a screenshot, a specific behaviour.

## Where it appears

Home page, features overview, brochure, README. Different surfaces want different lengths.

## Tone check

Consistent with `../../showcase/branding/README.md`. Confident, specific, no superlatives that
cannot be backed up.
