# <What happened> — <date>

## What happened

Plainly, in one or two sentences.

## How it was noticed

Be honest here. "Found by accident while looking at something else" is a finding in itself — it
means nothing was watching, which is usually the more important problem.

## Impact

What it cost: money, data, time, or availability. Include "none, but it could have" where true.

## Why it happened

The actual cause, not the trigger. "The teardown step is convention and convention lost" is a
cause; "someone forgot" is a trigger.

## What was done

The immediate fix, and how it was verified against reality rather than against a script's exit
code.

## What stops it recurring

The honest answer is often "nothing yet". Say that, and raise an issue, rather than writing an
intention nobody will act on.

## Related

Issues, commits, designs.
